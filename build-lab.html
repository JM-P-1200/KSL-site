FAQ

Plain answers first.

Is this a coding course?

No. It is a guided project workspace for launching and improving a website business. Coding can be one build method, but it is not required.

Can beginners use it?

Yes. The language and tasks assume the learner may be launching their first serious website.

Will this guarantee income or Google rankings?

No. Build Lab avoids unverified promises. It helps you build better assets and workflows, but results depend on demand, execution, competition, and time.
