KSL helps people choose an AI or no-code builder without opening thirty tabs. We organize tools around real jobs, explain the tradeoffs plainly, and keep paid influence out of the ranking.

Why KSL exists

The website-building landscape has become astonishingly capable—and unusually noisy. Feature lists rarely tell you whether a tool will fit your skills, your deadline, or the thing you’re trying to ship.

KSL began as a builder’s notebook in the mountains of Northern Luzon. Today, it is becoming a practical field guide: small enough to trust, broad enough to discover something useful, and honest enough to tell you when a popular tool is the wrong choice.
