Privacy notice

Draft for owner review — not approved for production.

Before release, confirm the operator's identity, publish a business contact address, and approve the retention policy below. Prepared October 28, 2026; no effective date has been set.

Who handles your information

KSL operates this website and handles its service inquiries, tool submissions, and newsletter requests.

Operator's legal name: pending owner confirmation.
Privacy contact: business email pending owner confirmation. This draft does not yet provide an active privacy-request address.

What we collect

Service inquiries contain your name, email, selected service, message, consent, preferred timeline, and any optional phone number or website URL you provide. Tool submissions contain contact details and the product information you send. Newsletter requests contain your email address.

Our hosting provider receives request metadata, which can include your IP address and browser information. To reduce spam, the application temporarily processes IP addresses and email addresses as keyed fingerprints with request counts; it does not retain the original values in its rate-limit store.

Please do not submit passwords, account access details, payment information, identity documents, or other sensitive personal information.

Why we use it

We use service-inquiry information to assess your request, prepare a quotation, and respond. Submitting an inquiry does not place an order or authorize payment. Tool information is used for editorial review. Newsletter emails are used for the updates you requested; a service inquiry does not subscribe you to marketing.

We ask for consent to process service inquiries for this purpose. Request metadata is also processed to secure the website and limit abuse. Automatic spam checks may temporarily reject a submission; they do not decide whether you qualify for a service.

How it is sent and who receives it

Forms are sent over HTTPS to KSL's backend on Vercel. Accepted requests are sent through Resend to KSL's configured email inbox, where the operator reviews and replies. These hosting and email providers process data to deliver their services, potentially outside the Philippines.

The application does not write inquiry messages into a customer database. Email copies still exist in the delivery service and receiving mailbox. Application diagnostics record submission type, provider status, and email reference IDs rather than the submitted message or contact details; hosting and delivery providers maintain their own operational records.

How long we keep it

Proposed policy, awaiting owner approval: review closed inquiries and unsuccessful quotations for deletion within 12 months of the last meaningful contact. If you become a client, keep only the records needed for the engagement and applicable recordkeeping or legal obligations. This requires manual mailbox and provider-record review; the website does not automatically erase those copies.

Temporary rate-limit fingerprints expire after at most 10 minutes and are removed on the next submission handled by that server instance, or when that instance is recycled. Hosting and email-provider records follow the relevant provider settings and policies. The operator must confirm those retention settings before this notice is published.

Your choices and rights

You can choose not to submit a form; without the required details, we may be unable to respond or assess the request. You may ask about your data, request access or correction, object to processing, withdraw consent, or request deletion or restriction where applicable. Some records may need to be kept for a legal obligation or claim. Withdrawal does not undo earlier lawful processing.

Send requests to the business contact address to be confirmed above, preferably from the address used for your inquiry. We may ask for proportionate information to verify the request. Do not send identity documents unless specifically needed and a suitable method has been agreed.

You may also raise a concern with the Philippine National Privacy Commission.

Other website features

Directory preferences and the Build Lab demo use your browser's local storage. Clearing browser site data removes those local copies. Some pages load Google Fonts, so the browser contacts Google to request font files. Third-party sites you visit through our directory have their own privacy notices.

This notice focuses on the website's current forms and features. We will update it before materially changing these uses.
